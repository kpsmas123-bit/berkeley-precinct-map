Berkeley precinct election results, 2014–2024

Each shape is a voting precinct. Pick an election and a contest, then color the map by who won, turnout, or a candidate's share. Click any precinct for its full results table. The map is focused on City Council District 1 (outlined), but you can click into any district.

A few things to know:

  • Gray precincts mean the contest wasn't on that ballot — not zero votes.
  • Ranked-choice contests show first-choice votes only (no precinct-level RCV rounds exist).
  • District lines changed in the 2021 redistricting, so "District 1" is a different area before vs. after.
  • Older elections had no certified precinct canvass; those totals are shown but marked "not independently reconciled."
  • Statewide propositions show only Berkeley's share, not the statewide result.
